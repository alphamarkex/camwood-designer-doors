01 / WHO WE ARE

Camwood Designer Doors & Windows

Camwood—also presented as Khusi Designer Doors & Windows—is a doors, windows and building-finishings business based in Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh. We provide a range of design references and product categories so homeowners, builders, renovators and project teams can compare styles and ask about suitable options.

Whether you are planning a main entrance, updating interior doors, selecting window frames or looking for compatible door hardware, our team can help you enquire about available products, finishes and sizing. Exact specifications and current availability should be confirmed with Camwood before placing an order.
